What helps public buildings stay useful during extreme heat?

Asked across the whole collection

Shaded approaches and external blinds can reduce heat before it reaches the building. Plans should also identify a cooler internal room and set clear thresholds for changing how a space is used.

The sources suggest that these changes work best when they are paired with local heat procedures, but they do not compare every building type.

Run the project on your terms

The complete application is published under the Apache 2.0 licence. One deployment can serve multiple organisation-specific portals, each shaped around its own research collection.

Open code deserves open working

The repository shows what exists, how it is checked and where work remains.

Developers, designers, researchers and documentation writers can report a problem or propose a change. Contributions are reviewed against the same standard as the product: clear behaviour, evidence where it matters, and tests for the change.

Known limits belong in the open. A useful contribution can be a careful bug report, clearer documentation, a stronger test or a focused improvement to the portal.
